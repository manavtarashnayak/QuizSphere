How It Works

Tailored experiences designed for students and educators alike.

For Students

Seamlessly attempt assigned assessments and track your mastery over time.

  • 1
    Browse & Select Quizzes

    Search available course quizzes filtered by topic, difficulty, or duration.

  • 2
    Take Distraction-Free Test

    Complete timed questions with real-time progress, mark for review, and instant navigation.

  • 3
    Review Detailed Analytics

    Get instant scores, percentage gauges, correct explanations, and attempt archives.

For Instructors

Easily author exams, manage questions, and analyze class performance.

  • 1
    Author Assessments

    Add questions dynamically, specify correct choices, and define passing thresholds.

  • 2
    Preview & Publish

    Review the quiz as a student before releasing it to your class or department.

  • 3
    Monitor Student Outcomes

    Inspect submission tallies, average scores, and individual attempt breakdowns.
